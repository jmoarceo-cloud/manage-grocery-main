# groceryone-thesis-evidence

Read [project context](../../../.ai/project-context.md) to locate this project's thesis/coursework and implementation evidence. Use the actual supplied institution/template if available; do not inherit it from another project.

1. Identify the requested academic artifact and its evidence stage: proposal, literature, design, method, results or defense. A proposal may describe planned work; results need completed observations.
2. Tie objectives and claims to actual requirements, source code, dated tests or participant records as applicable. A test filename or passing result from another project is not evidence here.
3. Verify literature against primary pages/papers and preserve bibliographic details and page/section support. Mark missing sources explicitly rather than generating plausible citations.
4. For methods, state actual or proposed participants, measures, consent/permissions and limits accurately. Never invent respondent counts, institutional approval, interviews, survey statistics, legal compliance or improved outcomes.
5. Write a bounded conclusion or draft with missing evidence visible, and update the existing literature/traceability records where they exist. Do not create an entire thesis folder for a small correction unless requested.

Project: groceryone. The portable AI setup itself provides no new business, application-test or user-study findings. Keep HCI lab deliverables distinct from the broader thesis manuscript.
